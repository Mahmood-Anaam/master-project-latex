## Method and findings

Methodological exposition: hierarchical goal/criteria/alternatives, reciprocal pairwise comparisons, relative priorities, consistency and group judgments. Illustrative drink consumption and job-choice examples.

## Critical appraisal and project relevance

AHP weights/priorities do not directly establish elapsed recovery times. Consistency does not prove contextual validity. Rating mode supports many alternatives but differs from pairwise relative mode. No BCM empirical validation claimed.

Project implications are review interpretations, not validated project results.
