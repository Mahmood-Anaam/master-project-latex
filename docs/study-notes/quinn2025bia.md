## Findings

Leader-approved mission, finance and reputation criteria; distinguishes BIA registers from risk registers; consequences conditional on disruption must not be confused with incident likelihood. Iterative top-down and bottom-up governance. Consistency is preferable to false numerical precision.

## Critical appraisal and project relevance
