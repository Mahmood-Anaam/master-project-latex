## Method and evidence

Full 21-page article read. Standards mapping, German expert questionnaire and public-sector pilot.

## Findings

22-process reference model; 47 invited/39 responding experts. BIA, recovery and exercises recognized by all respondents. Governance/core/support distinctions; documented requirements with rationale and executive approval.

## Critical appraisal and project relevance

Sections 3–5, Tables 1–5. Expert agreement supports relevance, not numerical recovery-rule validity. Prespecified list and one geographic region limit generalization; pilot benefits qualitative. Some below-consensus processes retained via standards interpretation. Fuzzy decision support remains future work, not implemented result.

The project implications above are reviewer interpretations, not claims made or validated by the proposed IS595 project.
