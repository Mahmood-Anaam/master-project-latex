## Findings

## Critical appraisal and project relevance

Sections 3–6.1. Relative propagation score is not absolute business tolerance or elapsed time. Offline activities outside logs excluded; log integrity assumed; single company/process; printed execution times include 66/63 seconds despite general under-minute narrative. Threshold 0.5 case-selected, not universal. Supports dependency checks and distinct evaluation of ranking versus recovery-time derivation.
