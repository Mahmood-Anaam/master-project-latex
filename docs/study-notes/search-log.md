# Search and selection record — 8 October 2026

This is a structured critical review, not an exhaustive systematic review. No PRISMA flow counts or claims of complete database coverage are made.

## Questions and search families

- BIA governance: business impact analysis, risk prioritization, time-dependent business impacts, RTO, MTPD.
- Analytical methods: business continuity testing points, multicriteria priority, analytic hierarchy process.
- Systems: business continuity information system, BCIN, PRIA, process mining impact propagation.
- Dependencies: critical societal services, lifeline recovery, restoration scheduling.
- Methods: design science evaluation, FEDS.

Primary publisher pages, NIST publications, institutional repositories and author-hosted full texts were consulted. DOI metadata was checked through Crossref where available. BCIN's legacy proceedings DOI is corroborated by indexed proceedings/publication records; its Crossref request returned 404 and the ACM page could not be retrieved. Publication years reflect the actual article, including 2025 journal volume for Shahverdi (online 2024) and 2016 volume for FEDS (online 2014).

## Inclusion

Nine full-text sources were read, then the chapter was drafted. Inclusion required directly useful methods, outputs or evaluation, and accessible complete text. Guidance reports, conceptual models, implemented prototypes and evaluated tools are distinguished. See source-manifest.json and each individual note.

- Other downloaded candidates (Kraus 2024, self-assessment 2025, criticality 2023, energy continuity 2025, integration 2022, adaptive restoration 2022, NIST 800-34, Hevner, Peffers, Miller): screened; full reading not completed for this chapter, therefore excluded from its synthesis and published study collection. ## Coverage limitations

Selection is targeted and affected by accessibility. No live commercial-product benchmark, Saudi regulatory-compliance assessment, exhaustive novelty claim, or project evaluation is inferred. Further literature may be added when directly needed for later authorized chapters, after full-text reading.
