## Method and findings

Formative/summative purposes are orthogonal to artificial/naturalistic settings. Four-step evaluation planning: goals, strategy, properties, episodes. Illustrations and one actual design-project application provide evidence of utility.

## Critical appraisal and project relevance

Evidence limited to small number of studies. Project should assess rule behavior, contextual fit and explanation separately; no claim that the proposed project has been evaluated.

Project implications are review interpretations, not validated project results.
